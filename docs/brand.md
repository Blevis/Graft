# Graft logo: "Reach"

A small green twig leans forward and reaches up and out. It has three leaves that alternate sides and grow towards the
tip. It stands for a new team member taking hold and growing towards working independently. There is no other detail.
The mark is built from four parts (a stem and three leaves), so it can be animated: the stem draws on, then each leaf
grows from where it joins the stem.

Concept round: `docs/brand/concepts-twig-green.png`. Presentation: `docs/brand/presentation.html` (slides in
`docs/brand/slides/`). All geometry is generated by `docs/brand/build_logo.py`. Edit the script, never the SVGs by
hand, then run `python docs/brand/build_logo.py`.

| `graft-animated.svg` | Stem as a centre-line stroke (`#stem`, `pathLength=1`), leaves `#leaf-1..3`, each with its joint as `transform-origin`. Pure CSS (0.7 s draw, then the leaves grow 0.18 s apart); respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. |
| `graft-lockup-light.svg` | Twig and ink wordmark, for light backgrounds (`#F6F5FA`, white). |
| `graft-lockup-dark.svg` | Twig and white wordmark, for the dark sidebar `#160E2E` and dark slides. |

Web icons (`frontend/public/icons/`, linked in `frontend/index.html`): `favicon.ico` (16/32/48), `favicon-*.png`,
`apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192/512.png`, `maskable-512.png`, `site.webmanifest`, and `graft-app-icon.svg` (green twig

The wordmark is Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold (800), converted to outlines. The font is under the SIL Open Font License,
which allows logo use.

## Colour

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Leaf green | `#2E9E5E` | 46 158 94 | The twig, always. 3.1:1 on `#F6F5FA`, 5.4:1 on `#160E2E` (graphics need 3:1). |
| Ink | `#211B35` | 33 27 53 | Wordmark on light backgrounds; one-colour print. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | Wordmark on dark; reversed one-colour mark. |

The twig is one flat colour, with no gradients, shadows or second green. The app's Orchid purple stays the interface
accent; the green belongs to the mark.

## Clear space and size
- Clear space: at least the length of the smallest leaf on every side. `graft-mark.svg` already includes it;
  `graft.svg` does not (the layout's gap provides it).
- In the lockups, the gap between twig and wordmark is fixed, as built. Don't respace it.
- Minimum size: regular cut 24 px; below that, use the small cut (made for 16 px). Lockup: 20 px tall.

## Motion
Start from `graft-animated.svg`. The stem draws from the root to the tip, then the leaves grow in order (lowest
first), each scaling from its joint. Keep the whole sequence under about 1.5 s and play it once (sign-in, first load,
empty states), never in a loop next to working content. With reduced motion, show the finished mark.

## Don't
- Add leaves, roots, a pot, a circle or a tile around the twig (the app icon tile is the only exception).
- Rotate, mirror or straighten the twig. The forward lean is the idea.
- Recolour single leaves, add outlines, gradients, glows or shadows.
- Use the regular cut below 24 px; switch to the small cut.
- Retype the wordmark or change its weight or spacing.

Trademark clearance has not been checked. Run a search (trademark databases, reverse image search) before
registering the mark.
